A little more about me

Dad first. Software engineer by trade. Happiest near the water.

The Short Version

I studied computer science at Gettysburg College and have spent about ten years building software since. I started out at Amazon Music, then ran a small technical operations team at Clean.io. After that I went back to building full time, first at HealthVerity and now at Capital One Software, optimizing our CI/CD pipelines to keep up with the ever increasing speed of AI.

Away from the Desk

Above all, I'm a dad, and that's where most of my time and energy go these days. Whatever's left goes to fly fishing, cold-water surfing, and playing guitar. Someday I'd like to start a breakfast burrito company. More on what I'm up to right now is on the Now page.
